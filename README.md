Absolutely. Based on the current **Waypoint — AI Job Search Copilot for Product Leaders** site, I would position the strongest features around **intelligent job selection and application strategy**, rather than presenting it as just another job-search portal. ([SachinBP100][1])

### ⭐ Best Features to Highlight

**1. AI-Powered Job Matching**
Waypoint goes beyond keyword matching by assessing how well a role aligns with a PM's **experience, skills, domain expertise, seniority and career goals**.

**2. Personalized Job Discovery**
Instead of forcing PMs to search through hundreds of listings, the product can surface opportunities that are most relevant to their individual profile and preferences.

**3. Job Fit Score**
A simple score can answer the most important question:

> **“How strong a candidate am I for this role?”**

The score should ideally be accompanied by *why* the candidate matches and where the gaps are.

**4. Role Intelligence**
AI can break down a job description into:

* Core responsibilities
* Must-have capabilities
* Preferred capabilities
* Seniority expectations
* Domain experience
* Technical expectations
* Leadership expectations
* Hidden signals in the JD

This is particularly valuable for senior PMs because two roles with the same title can have dramatically different scopes.

**5. Candidate–Role Gap Analysis**
One of the strongest differentiators could be:

> **“What am I missing for this role?”**

Waypoint can identify skill or experience gaps and distinguish between **true gaps** and requirements that are already supported by the candidate's experience.

**6. AI Resume Positioning**
Rather than generating a generic resume, Waypoint can tell the candidate:

> “For this particular role, these 4 experiences should be emphasized.”

This shifts AI from **resume generation → strategic positioning**.

**7. Application Copilot**
Once a candidate selects a role, Waypoint can help create:

* Tailored resume content
* Cover letter
* Recruiter message
* Application responses
* “Why are you interested?” answers
* “Why are you a good fit?” responses

**8. Interview Preparation**
The same job intelligence can flow into interview preparation:

**Job Description → Requirements → Candidate Experience → Likely Questions → Best Stories**

This creates a much stronger end-to-end experience than a standalone job board.

**9. PM Career Profile / Experience Library**
A powerful long-term feature would be a reusable **PM Evidence Library** containing:

* Products owned
* Business outcomes
* Metrics
* Leadership examples
* Product launches
* Technical projects
* Domain expertise
* STAR stories

AI can then retrieve the most relevant evidence for every job.

**10. Job Search Analytics**
Waypoint can turn the job search into a measurable funnel:

**Jobs discovered → Relevant → Shortlisted → Applied → Recruiter response → Interview → Offer**

This allows the PM to understand **what is actually working**.

### The 3 Features I Would Make the Hero Differentiators

If you're briefing other Product Managers, I'd emphasize these three:

| Feature                                | PM Value                      |
| -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| **🎯 AI Fit Score**                    | Should I apply?               |
| **🔍 Gap & Positioning Intelligence**  | How should I position myself? |
| **🚀 Application + Interview Copilot** | What should I do next?        |

Together they create a compelling product proposition:

> **Waypoint doesn't just help Product Managers find jobs. It helps them decide which opportunities are worth pursuing, understand how they fit, position themselves effectively, and prepare to win the opportunity.**

### The bigger product opportunity

I would ultimately evolve Waypoint from **“AI Job Search Copilot”** into a **“Career Intelligence Platform for Product Leaders.”**

The strategic journey becomes:

**Discover → Evaluate → Position → Apply → Prepare → Interview → Learn → Improve**

That gives you a much stronger product story and a substantially larger roadmap than a traditional job-search application.
